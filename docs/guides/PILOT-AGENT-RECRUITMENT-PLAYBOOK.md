# Pilot Agent Recruitment Playbook
**Goal:** Recruit 3 FUB-using real estate agents for free LeadFlow pilot (Ontario/Canadian market)**
**Approach:** White-glove — personal LinkedIn DM + setup call offer
**Day:** 43 of 90 (MRR target: $20K)

---

## Target Profile (ICP)

- Real estate agent in Ontario (Toronto, GTA, Hamilton, Ottawa)
- Actively using Follow Up Boss (FUB) CRM
- Solo agent or small team (1–5 agents)
- Min 2 years active — has a lead volume problem, not a "getting started" problem
- Pain point: slow lead response time, leads going cold before they pick up the phone

**How to find them on LinkedIn:**
- Search: `real estate agent Follow Up Boss Ontario`
- Search: `realtor FUB Ontario Toronto`
- Look for agents posting about lead gen, expired listings, online leads (Zillow/Realtor.ca)
- Check FUB's own LinkedIn followers/community posts — real users comment there

## Call Script (15-min Setup Call)

**Opening (2 min)**
> "Thanks for your time. I'll be quick — I built LeadFlow to solve a specific problem: agents on FUB getting leads they can't respond to fast enough. Every minute you wait, the lead calls someone else."

**Demo hook (3 min)**
> "We've seen agents go from 40-minute average response to under 30 seconds. The AI sends a personalized SMS from your number, qualifies the lead, and can book a call via Cal.com — all while you're with another client."

**The pilot offer (3 min)**
> "I'm looking for 3 agents to pilot this for free. That means:
> - I connect LeadFlow to your FUB account
> - Set up your AI persona and SMS number
> - You run it for 30 days — no cost, no contract
> - If you love it, we talk pricing. If not, we part ways clean."

**Objection handling:**
- *"I'm not tech-savvy"* → "That's exactly why I'm doing white-glove — I do the setup. You just answer your phone when leads call back."
- *"I already have a VA"* → "The AI covers the 2am leads, the Sunday afternoon floods. Your VA can't be on 24/7."
- *"Does it sound robotic?"* → "Let me send you an example SMS thread right now."

**Close (2 min)**
> "So — can I get you onboarded this week? Takes about 30 minutes on my end. You'll be live before your next lead comes in."

## Onboarding Checklist (per pilot agent)

Once they say yes, complete in 48 hours:

- [ ] Get their FUB API key (`Settings > Integrations > API` in FUB)
- [ ] Provision a Twilio number (or use their existing number if they have one)
- [ ] Set up agent record in LeadFlow dashboard
- [ ] Configure AI persona (name, tone, key talking points)
- [ ] Enable Cal.com booking link (or skip for now if they don't use it)
- [ ] Send them the "you're live" message with their dedicated SMS number
- [ ] Schedule a 1-week check-in call

**Setup time target:** <45 minutes per agent
