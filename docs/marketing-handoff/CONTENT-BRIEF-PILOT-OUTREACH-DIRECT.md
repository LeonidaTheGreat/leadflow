**Why personal outreach, not a campaign:**
- No inbound traffic exists yet. Campaigns need audiences; Stojan already knows these people.

This disarms sales resistance immediately. Stojan is NOT asking them to buy. He is asking them to help him make something better. That is a fundamentally different — and far easier — conversation.

## Objection Handling Cheat Sheet

**"I'm too busy right now."**
> Totally fine. Can I check back in 2 weeks, or is there a better time?
> *(Keep the door open. Don't push. Most busy objections are "not yet", not "no".)*

**"I already have a tool that does follow-up."**
> What are you using? *(Listen.)* Most tools send an email 30 minutes later. This sends an SMS in under 30 seconds and holds a real conversation. Happy to show you the difference — just 15 minutes.

**"How much does it cost?"**
> Nothing for the pilot — it's completely free. After the pilot, if you want to keep it, it's $49-149/month. But there's no obligation and no credit card for the test.

**"What do you need from me to set it up?"**
> Just your Follow Up Boss API key and a Cal.com account (free to create if you don't have one). I handle the configuration. Takes about 5 minutes on your end, then we test it live against a real lead.

**"Is this TCPA compliant?"**
> Yes — built-in opt-out handling, quiet hours (9 PM–8 AM), and consent tracking baked in. I take compliance seriously because I know agents can't afford not to.

**"Will it sound robotic?"**
> That's the most common concern. The honest answer: your leads won't know. The AI is trained on real estate conversations specifically. If you're skeptical, the demo will settle it — you'll see a real conversation play out.
